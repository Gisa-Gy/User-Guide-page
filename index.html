Land Use Compliance & Enforcement Platform

How to use this hub

Everything in this hub follows one case, from the moment a change is spotted on satellite imagery to the day the case is closed. This page explains that path and which screen belongs to your role.

The case workflow

Five stages. Each one is carried out by a different team, and each one writes to the same case record.

1
A change is detected A land use change is detected from satellite imagery and recorded as a detection point.
2
An inspector visits the site The inspector completes the Field Inspection Form. A compliance case is created automatically with a Case ID in the form VNP-ZONE-YEAR-NUMBER.
3
A supervisor decides The supervisor reviews the inspection and records a determination and a decision.
4
Enforcement acts An enforcement officer serves the action, records the deadline and closes the case once it is resolved.
5
The trail is kept Every status change is written to the case history record.

Which dashboard to open

All four read the same case record. They differ in what they put in front of you.

Management & partners

Executive Overview

Regional totals, status breakdown and trend.

Detection & inspection

Reported Cases

What has been reported and what inspectors found.

Officers & supervisors

Case Management

The review queue and the decision record.

Enforcement, legal, NLA, RDB

Enforcement & Resolution

Actions served, deadlines, overdue items and resolutions.

Working with the dashboards

Filters

Each dashboard has a filter panel on its right edge. Click the arrow to slide it open. Filters cover district, sector, cell, village, case identifier and a date range, and they apply to every element on that dashboard at once.

Entering data

Data is entered only through the forms on this hub or through ArcGIS Field Maps on a phone. Map layers are not editable, so a record cannot be changed by clicking the map.

Access

This hub is shared within the organisation. A user only sees the cases and dashboards their portal account is entitled to see.
